Method and limitations

This prototype uses standardized Linear Regression as an interpretable baseline. A rule-based explanation layer flags potentially relevant irradiation, temperature, wind, and outage conditions. These are indicators, not proof of causality.

  • Only 60 dummy daily records are available.
  • Model quality may vary with this small dataset; compare MAE and RMSE rather than relying on R² alone.
  • Production use requires more historical data, asset-specific details, weather forecasts, and maintenance/curtailment logs.
